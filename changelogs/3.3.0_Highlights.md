# **Modpack Patch Notes: Version 3.3.0**
**Release Date:** November 29th, 2026

### *The Revival Update*

## **Overview**
Version **3.3.0** revives *Genesis* with **Create 6.0**, a wave of new Create addons, a rebuilt questbook and a switch from **EMI** to **JEI**. Almost every mod in the pack has been updated, the pack's scripts have been reorganized, and custom items now use their own `genesis:` ids, with existing items converted automatically so nothing is lost.

---

## **Key Highlights**
- **Create 6.0**: Updated Create from v0.5.1 to v6.0.8.1, bringing package logistics, chain conveyors, stock tickers and more, with new quests to guide you through them.
- **New Create Addons**: Added Create: Connected, Create: Dragons Plus, Create Contraption Terminals, Create Crafts & (More) Additions and Create: Jetpack Curios, and updated Create: Enchantment Industry to v2.5.4.
- **Create Foundry Moulds**: Every molten metal can now be cast in **Ingot**, **Gem** and **Block Moulds**, including Advanced Netherite, Ad Astra and Better End metals. Block Moulds replace the old molten-to-block compacting recipes.
- **Better Storage**: Added Sophisticated Backpacks, Sophisticated Storage and Sophisticated Storage In Motion, plus Simple Magnets, More Barrel Variants and More Bookshelf Variants.
- **JEI Replaces EMI**: EMI has been replaced by **Just Enough Items** and **JEI++**, which brings back the recipe tree, while loading recipes only once for faster load times.
- **Questbook Overhaul**: Reworked every chapter with better rewards, descriptions and layouts, added **Sophisticated**, **Travelers Backpacks** and **Decoration** chapters, and reordered the chapters to follow progression.
- **New Structures**: Added Create: Ultimate Factory, Create: Sky Village, Create: Rustic Structures, Create: Structures Overhaul, and new Ad Astra and Deeper and Darker structure packs.
- **Quality of Life & Performance**: Added Journeymap, Liteminer, Inventory Management, Mouse Tweaks, Crafting Tweaks, Construction Wand and Farming for Blockheads, plus BadOptimizations, Enhanced Block Entities and SmoothGL.
- **Visual Refresh**: Updated Complementary Shaders, split Fresh Animations into individual extensions, added new resource packs, and added a new FancyMenu slideshow and loading screen.
- **Safe ID Migration**: Custom items, blocks and fluids moved from `kubejs:` to `genesis:` ids, and old items in inventories, Ender Chests and containers convert to the new versions automatically.

---

#### **⚠️ Updating an Existing World?**

- **Back up your world first.** This update makes major changes to Create and many of its addons.
- Old `kubejs:` items convert automatically when you log in, open a container or right-click it. Items in storage the scripts can't reach can be crafted into their new versions.
- Fluids from the old `kubejs:` molten metals can be converted by running them through a Mechanical Mixer.
